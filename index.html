Privacy Policy for FileUpgrader

Last Updated: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FileUpgrader ("the Software"), a Autodesk® Revit® add-in developed by Haluk Uzuner, handles information. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected.

1. Data Collection and Usage

No Personal Data: The Software does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, such as names, email addresses, or contact details.

Local Processing: All file processing (upgrading Autodesk® Revit® projects and families) is performed entirely on your local machine. The Software does not upload your Autodesk® Revit® files (.rvt, .rfa) to any external servers or cloud storage.

No Tracking: We do not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technologies to monitor your usage of the Software.

2. Permissions

The Software requires the following permissions to function:

  • File System Access: To read the files you select for upgrading and to save the upgraded versions to your specified destination folder.
  • Autodesk® Revit® API Access: To interact with the Autodesk® Revit® environment for the purpose of upgrading file versions.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. Since no data is collected, no data is shared.

4. Security

As the Software operates locally on your workstation, the security of your files remains within your own local network and IT infrastructure.
